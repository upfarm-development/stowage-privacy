# Stowage Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Stowage is a packing-list app. It does not collect, receive or share your data.

## What Stowage stores

Your trips, packing items, categories, bags and settings. They are stored on your
device.

## iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Stowage syncs your trips and settings between
your own devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit and the iCloud key-value store).
This data goes to your private iCloud account. It is not sent to the developer,
and the developer cannot read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by
Apple's privacy policy. You can turn sync off in iOS Settings under your name,
then iCloud.

## Siri and Shortcuts

Stowage lets Siri and Shortcuts read and change your packing lists. This happens
on your device, and Stowage does not receive what you say. Siri itself is
covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## What Stowage does not do

- No accounts, sign-in or servers run by the developer.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
- No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

## Children

Stowage collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
